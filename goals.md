# Goals

## Flagship Goal
Ship Cyber Lazer Mermicorn — Drop 001 as a coherent three-look capsule that demonstrates creative commerce capability.

## Drop 001 Target
Theme: bioluminescent ocean × cyber fantasy × Honolulu nightlife  
1. Hero bodysuit or two-piece set  
2. Modular shrug / sleeves / hood  
3. Unisex accessory or utility piece  

Each design: front/back/side concepts, three colorways, materials & trims, lighting effects, production difficulty, tech-pack draft, manufacturer questions, price tier, product page copy, social assets.

## Success
A complete production-and-launch pack that can be handed to a sewing partner and used as a portfolio case study.
