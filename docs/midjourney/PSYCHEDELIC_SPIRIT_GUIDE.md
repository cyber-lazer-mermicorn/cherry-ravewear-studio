# Psychedelic–Spiritual Design Guide (Community-Respectful)

For humans who know the floor: the best “spiritual” rave moments are usually
**simple** — a bassline, a color change, a stranger’s smile, the feeling of being
safe enough to open.

This guide keeps Cherry’s design language in that lane.

---

## Foundation stack

```text
BEAT → BODY → LIGHT → COLOR → JOY → (optional) MEANING
```

If meaning is added before beat and body, it reads as costume.

---

## What the community often loves (and supports)

1. **Safety and consent energy** in the image — relaxed faces, open posture, no menace
2. **Inclusive bodies** — not one mannequin ideal
3. **Light as shared language** — LEDs, lasers, UV as emotion not clutter
4. **Color with intention** — limited palette, breathing pairs
5. **Fabric that can move** — mesh, stretch, flow, reflective edge
6. **Play without cruelty** — humor welcome; humiliation never
7. **Afterglow** — the soft hour, not only peak face-melt
8. **Nature-adjacent nights** when honest — trees, dome, shore — not fake temple theft

---

## Visual vocabulary that works

| Use | Why |
|-----|-----|
| Breathing two-hue light | Feels alive with the beat |
| Soft radial glow behind subject | Awe without a stamped mandala |
| Haze + single laser plane | Depth, not chaos |
| Reflective edge only | Discipline; reads premium and intentional |
| Eyes soft / closed in feeling | Inward joy, not performance of holiness |
| Hands open | Welcome, release |
| Grounded stance | Beat in the feet |

---

## Visual vocabulary to refuse

| Avoid | Why |
|-------|-----|
| Random sacred geometry decals | Usually empty or appropriative |
| Stolen ceremonial dress cues | Harm + fake |
| Drug-step instructional imagery | Not our lane; legal/ethical risk |
| “Shaman” stereotype kits | Lazy and disrespectful |
| Every neon at maximum | Exhausting; not floor-elegant |
| Logo soup | Breaks immersion |

---

## Collection concepts that fit this ethic

- **Neon Tide** — water/light, no mermaid cliché, tidal panels
- **Afterglow** — soft post-peak, violet/teal breath
- **Signal** — reflective edge as communication, minimal body, max clarity
- **Hearth Floor** — warm practicals + cool LEDs, community intimacy
- **Dawn Gate** — leaving the night, pastel shift, gratitude tone

Each needs: beat-readable movement, honest materials, partner-sew reality.

---

## Copy tone (matches the images)

Good:
> Built for the floor. Light on the seams. Room to move.

Good:
> Design by Cherry. Made with partners.

Avoid:
> Shamanic portal activation suit

Avoid:
> Guaranteed enlightenment fabric

---

## Integration with design packages

When a package leans spiritual:
1. Put the feeling words in `concept.mood`
2. Keep silhouette and materials practical
3. Use Prompt Bank recipes 6 or 9, not sacred-symbol spam
4. List uncertainty if a symbol was ever considered and rejected
